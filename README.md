# AgriTech

A Telegram-based agricultural chatbot designed to help farmers make smarter decisions by estimating yield, sharing efficient farming practices, and answering crop and machinery questions.

## Features

- **Yield estimation**
  - Collects information about land, soil type, crop sown, irrigation methods, fertilizers used, climate conditions, and more.
  - Calculates an estimated crop yield and efficiency percentage based on the farmer's inputs.

- **Farming guidance**
  - Provides farmers with recommendations for efficient farming practices.
  - Suggests ways to implement those practices to improve productivity and reduce waste.

- **Farmer support**
  - Answers questions about farming methods, crop management, and agricultural machinery.
  - Helps farmers better understand how to grow crops and maintain equipment.

## How it works

1. The farmer interacts with the bot through Telegram.
2. The bot asks for details about farmland, soil, crop, irrigation, fertilizer, and climate.
3. It uses that information to estimate yield and determine efficiency.
4. The bot can also provide tailored advice and answer farming-related questions.

## Benefits

- Helps farmers make data-driven decisions.
- Improves crop planning and resource use.
- Offers practical, easy-to-follow farming tips.
- Supports farmers with fast answers about crops and machinery.

## Usage

- Install and configure the Telegram bot with your bot token.
- Deploy the project to a server or bot hosting platform.
- Share the bot with farmers so they can begin submitting field details and asking questions.

## Notes

- The accuracy of yield estimates depends on the quality and completeness of the input data.
- Recommendations are meant to support decision-making and should be combined with local agricultural expertise.
